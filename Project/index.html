About Roshni Public School & College

At Roshni Public School & College, Gogdara, we believe true education is the spark that illuminates young minds, builds confidence, and strengthens communities. Located in Gogdara, Swat, our campus offers a safe, modern learning environment for students from primary through college level — combining strong academics with character, discipline, and leadership.

What sets us apart in Gogdara, Swat.

Our mission

To deliver accessible, quality education that builds academic excellence and strong moral character — preparing students for higher education and responsible citizenship.
